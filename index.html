01 — Why Fresh Lines

Paint is the easy part.
The prep is the job.

Anyone can sell you a coat of paint. We sell the hours before it: the filling, sanding, masking and priming that decide how the room looks in two years.

Fresh Lines is owner-run by Kristina Marie, with two crews working out of Irmo and a 40-mile radius across the Midlands. Eight years in the trade, the last few building a reputation here the old way — one finished room at a time.

You get a written scope, a start date, and the same person answering the phone from the walkthrough to the final touch-up.

04 — The process

Four steps.
No surprises.

You always know who is coming, what happens that day, and what it costs.

  1. 01

    Walkthrough

    We look at the space, the surface and the light. Measurements and photos, fifteen minutes.

  2. 02

    Written quote

    A fixed, line-item number: labour, materials, prep and the days we will be there.

  3. 03

    Prep & protect

    Furniture covered, floors protected, cracks filled, surfaces sanded and primed.

  4. 04

    Paint & walk

    Finish coats, clean edges, clean-up, then a walkthrough with touch-ups before we leave.
