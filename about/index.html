About

Owner-run.
Two crews. One standard.

Fresh Lines Painting is Kristina Marie's company, working out of Irmo across the South Carolina Midlands.

The story

Eight years in Ohio.
A fresh start in Irmo.

Kristina ran a painting company in Ohio for eight years before relocating to South Carolina. She brought the crews, the standards and the habit of answering her own phone.

Fresh Lines is deliberately small: two crews, a 40-mile radius, and a calendar that is not overbooked. That is what makes it possible to finish a job before starting the next one, and to come back if something is not right.

The work is mostly repeat and referral — realtors, property managers and homeowners who saw a room at a friend's house. There is no call centre and no sales script. You talk to the person who will walk the job.

What we believe

Three rules we
do not bend.

  • 01

    Prep is not optional

    If a surface is not ready, paint will not fix it. We would rather add a day than hand over a finish that fails in a year.

  • 02

    The quote is the quote

    No surprise line items at the end. If we missed something, that is our problem to solve, not a new invoice for you.

  • 03

    Leave it cleaner

    Every day ends with a vacuum and a clear path through the house. The job is not finished until the room is usable.

Where we work
