Process

Four steps, then
the good part.

Painting goes wrong in the gaps — between the quote and the start date, between one trade and the next. We closed them.

  1. 01

    Walkthrough & measure

    Fifteen minutes in the room, not a phone estimate. We check the surface, the light, the trim and what needs patching. You get a straight answer about what can be saved and what needs replacing.

  2. 02

    Written, fixed quote

    Labour, materials, prep and the number of days, line by line. You approve a start date before anything is ordered. If something changes the scope, you get a change order before we proceed.

  3. 03

    Prep, protect, prime

    Furniture moved and covered. Floors protected. Cracks and nail pops filled, surfaces sanded, joints caulked and bare spots primed. This is where the finish is won.

  4. 04

    Finish & walkthrough

    Two finish coats, cut lines by hand, hardware and furniture back. We walk the space together, mark anything that needs attention, and leave touch-up paint labelled for the future.

What you get

A clean job,
not just clean walls.

Daily clean-up is part of the quote, not a favour. Tools down, floors vacuumed, furniture roughly back before we leave each day, so the house stays livable while we work.
